1. List all 6 phases of SDLC in order with one line about each.

    1.Requirement 
       – In this phase, we understand what the client or user needs from the software.

    2.Design 
       – The structure, UI and overall design of the software are planned.

    3.Development 
       – Developers write the code and build the software.

    4.Testing 
       – The software is tested to find bugs and check whether it works properly.

    5.Deployment 
       – The completed software is released and made available to users.

    6.Maintenance 
       – After release, bugs are fixed and new changes or improvements are added.



2. Write 3 differences between Agile and Waterfall models.


    1.Agile

      - Work is done in small iterations and changes can be made easily.
      - Customer feedback can be taken regularly during development.
      - Testing and development can happen repeatedly during the project.


    2.Waterfall

       - Work is completed phase by phase in a fixed order.
       - Customer feedback usually comes after major stages or development.
       - Testing generally comes after the development phase.



3. Name the 3 main Scrum roles and write one-line responsibility of each.


   1.Product Owner 
      – Manages the product requirements and decides what features are most important.

   2.Scrum Master 
      – Helps the team follow Scrum and removes problems that affect the team's work.

   3.Development Team 
      – Builds, tests and delivers the product features.



4. What is a Sprint in Scrum? Mention its typical duration.



    - A Sprint is a fixed period in which the Scrum team works on selected tasks and tries to complete them.

      Typical duration: 2 weeks.



5. What do these Jira board columns mean: To Do, In Progress, Done, Backlog?


    1.To Do 
      – Tasks that are planned but work has not started yet.

    2.In Progress 
      – Tasks that are currently being worked on.

    3.Done 
      – Tasks that have been completed.

    4.Backlog 
       – A list of tasks or requirements that are planned for future work.
